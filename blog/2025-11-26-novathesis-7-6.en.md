Two additions in this release reach well beyond any single school.

## SDG icons

Theses increasingly state which **Sustainable Development Goals** the work
contributes to. The template can now print the official SDG icons on the
abstract page, in English or Portuguese, in the normal, inverted or monochrome
style.

The icons come from **[joaomlourenco/SDGs](https://github.com/joaomlourenco/SDGs)**,
a companion repository of high-quality vector (SVG and PDF) versions of the
goals in seven languages. From 8.1.0 onwards the template downloads the set it
needs on demand, so nothing ships in the template itself.

## Seven new languages

Covers and fixed strings gained **Danish (`dk`), Catalan (`cat`), Czech
(`cz`), Slovak (`sk`), Polish (`pl`) and Dutch (`nl`)**, among others — useful
for a cotutelle, an exchange, or a jury that reads in another language.

Also new: the `palatino`, `palatino-gyre-pagella` and `palatino-linotype` font
styles, and school configurations standardised into `*-defaults` files, which
is what makes adding a school a matter of one file rather than several.
