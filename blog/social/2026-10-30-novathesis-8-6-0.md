### LinkedIn — EN

novathesis 8.6.0 is out, and it adds Atlântica — Instituto Universitário to the schools the template supports.

The release is codenamed "Albatross: Crossing the Atlantic", which is a small joke twice over: the school is called Atlântica, and the first degree supported there is the BSc in Ciências da Engenharia Aeronáutica. Students on that course can now produce a thesis that follows their faculty's rules without having to discover those rules by trial and error.

Getting a school right is rarely a matter of reading one document. Atlântica's "Normas para Apresentação e Discussão do Projecto Final de Licenciatura" dates from 2019 and, like most such regulations, leaves plenty unsaid. The gaps were filled from a dozen real Atlântica theses: A4, two-sided, Times New Roman at 12pt with 1.5 line spacing, specific margins, the title and degree in the header, the author and date in the footer, chapters opening on right-hand pages.

That requirement for Times New Roman turned out to matter well beyond Atlântica. The template has offered a "times" font style for years, and under modern engines it needed Times New Roman, Helvetica and Courier New to be installed on the machine — which, on Overleaf and on most Linux systems, they are not. Anyone whose faculty mandates Times had a style that simply did not compile. Each missing font is now replaced by its metric-compatible TeX Gyre equivalent, which ships with every TeX Live installation. Metric-compatible is the important word: the substitute occupies exactly the space the original would, so nothing on the page moves.

If your institution is not yet supported, that is the most useful thing anyone can do with this post: open a "New school" issue with your faculty's formatting rules and media kit. Almost every school in the template arrived that way — contributed by a student who had their own dissertation to finish.

## Reddit — r/LaTeX (title + body; no hashtags)

**Title:** novathesis 8.6.0: style/font=times no longer needs Times New Roman installed (falls back to TeX Gyre), plus a new school

**Body:**

Two things in this release that might be useful here.

**The one that matters beyond our users:** the template's `times` font style required Times New Roman, Helvetica and Courier New to be installed when compiled with LuaLaTeX or XeLaTeX. On Overleaf and most Linux systems they aren't, so `style/font=times` failed outright — awkward, given how many faculties mandate Times. Each missing font is now replaced independently by its metric-compatible TeX Gyre clone from TeX Live (Termes, Heros, Cursor), so line and page breaks are unchanged, and the colophon reports the fonts actually used rather than the ones requested.

**The new school:** Atlântica — Instituto Universitário (`school=atlantica/cea`), for the BSc in Ciências da Engenharia Aeronáutica — hence the release codename, "Albatross: Crossing the Atlantic". Built from the school's 2019 formatting regulation plus a dozen real theses for everything the regulation doesn't say. Atlântica's rules are what forced the Times fix above.

Also new: `\SetAuthorStudentNumber*()={12345}` puts the student number on the cover. Only Atlântica's covers render it; it's accepted and ignored elsewhere.
