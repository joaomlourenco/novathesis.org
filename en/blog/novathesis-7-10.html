novathesis 7.10 — Chinese abstracts, and ISEL

Abstracts in Simplified and Traditional Chinese, support for IPL/ISEL, and automated integrity statements for NOVA FCSH.

The last 7.x line, and the one that took the template outside the Latin script for the first time.

Abstracts in Chinese

Full support for abstracts in Simplified (zhs) and Traditional (zht) Chinese, with the CJK font loading tuned so it does not slow every other build down.

IPL/ISEL

Instituto Superior de Engenharia de Lisboa arrived with its own styles, cover layouts and integrity statements. NOVA FCSH had its integrity statement automated in the same release.

Dates and statements

Date handling was overhauled and standardised on \PrintDateISO across schools, and statement generation — integrity, copyright — was refactored for consistency. If you have ever seen a cover print a date in one format and a statement in another, this is the release that stopped it.

Version 8.0 followed a few months later, and with it the move of glossaries to bib2gls.
