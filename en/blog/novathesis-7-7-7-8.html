novathesis 7.7 and 7.8 — Declaring AI use, and three more ULisboa schools

A formal AI-usage declaration for your thesis, support for FCUL, IST and ISEG, and a new spine design.

December brought the feature that has aged best, and a good deal of new school support.

Declaring how you used AI

Universities increasingly expect a statement about generative-AI use in a thesis. The template integrates the aidisclose package, so the declaration is part of the document rather than something you improvise at the end.

Three more ULisboa schools

FCUL (Faculdade de Ciências), IST (Instituto Superior Técnico) and ISEG (Lisbon School of Economics and Management) joined the supported list, and most schools received a new spine design.

Under the hood

stocksize.sty was rewritten in expl3, and page layout moved to the geometry package instead of memoir's own mechanism — dull from the outside, but it is what makes unusual paper sizes and margins behave predictably.
