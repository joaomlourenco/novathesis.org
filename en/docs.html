Documentation

The short version lives here: what each configuration file does and where to change it. Everything deeper is in the wiki.

0-Config/1_novathesis.tex

Document type and school

Choose phd, msc or bsc, select your school and language, and set the template's main options.

0-Config/2_biblatex.tex

Bibliography engine

biblatex and biber settings: citation style, sorting, and how references are printed.

0-Config/3_cover.tex

Cover and title data

Title and subtitle per language, author, supervisors, jury, degree and dates — everything printed on the cover and spine.

0-Config/4_files.tex

Your files

Which .tex chapters, appendices and .bib files make up the document, and in what order.

0-Config/5_packages.tex

Extra packages

Add your own LaTeX packages and macros without touching the class file.

0-Config/7-aidisclose.tex

AI disclosure

The generative-AI disclosure statement; the snippet can be generated at aidisclose.org and pasted here.

The full documentation is the wiki

Options reference, per-school notes, migration guides and troubleshooting, kept alongside the source.
