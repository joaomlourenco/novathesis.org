Find your school

Each repository is a ready-to-use snapshot of the main template, pre-configured for one institution and kept automatically in sync. Recognise your cover, take the repository.

Overleaf imports the repository into a new Overleaf project — this needs a paid plan, as the template does not compile within the Free plan's 20-second limit. A free alternative is ScienHub, where you upload the ZIP yourself.
